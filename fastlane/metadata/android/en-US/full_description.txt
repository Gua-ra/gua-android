Gua is private messaging without the walled garden.

Simple and secure: send messages, share photos, videos and files, and keep up with the people who matter, all protected with end-to-end encryption by default. Your conversations stay between you and the people you are talking to.

Why Gua

 • End-to-end encrypted by default. Messages are encrypted on your device, so no one in between can read them.
 • Sign in with your phone number and start talking in seconds.
 • Find people you already know who are on Gua, and start a private chat or a group.
 • Rich messaging: replies, reactions, edits, voice messages, and photo, video and file sharing.
 • Built on an open, federated network, so no single company owns your communication.

<b>Private by design</b>
Gua does not mine your conversations and does not sell your data. Encryption is on from the very first message, with nothing to set up. You are not the product.

<b>Open and verifiable</b>
Gua is open source and built on an open, federated network. Anyone can study how it works and check how their data is handled. Organizations can run their own trusted servers within the network, so communities and institutions can keep control of their own data.

<b>Permissions</b>
 • Contacts: used to help you find people you already know who are on Gua. Your contact details are protected before they leave your device.
 • Camera and microphone: used to capture and send photos, videos and voice messages.
 • Notifications: used to let you know about new messages, including on your lock screen.

Gua is in active development and we are adding new features all the time. We would love your feedback.
